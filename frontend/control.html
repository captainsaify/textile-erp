Correct a line on a bill

Changing the item or its label moves the stock with it and recosts both products — a relabelled line is a different product, not an edited field.

Adjust stock

A typed movement, never an edit of the balance: the balance is worked out from movements, and writing it directly makes the two disagree.

Rebuild costs

Replays every average from movement history. Safe to run: it computes rather than destroys, and rolls back if anything stops balancing.
